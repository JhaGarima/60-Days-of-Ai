## 📌 Overview

**Media Integrity Analyzer** is an interactive educational web application that teaches users how to critically evaluate digital media through guided discovery rather than traditional quizzes.

Instead of testing prior knowledge, the application presents fictional headlines, articles, social posts, and media scenarios that encourage users to pause, observe, think critically, and then compare their conclusions with a detailed analysis.

The experience mirrors how misinformation appears in everyday life while helping users develop practical media literacy skills that can be applied across news websites, social media platforms, and online discussions.

## 🎯 Learning Experience

### Challenge 1: Headline Detective

Learn how misleading headlines influence clicks.

Users will:

- Read a fictional news headline and article
- Decide whether they would click it
- Identify exaggerated or misleading wording
- Reveal:
  - Headline Accuracy Score
  - Highlighted mismatches
  - Explanation
  - Fair rewritten headline
  - Practical takeaway

---

### Challenge 2: Emotion Detector

Understand how emotional language influences perception.

Users will:

- Read a fictional social media post or article excerpt
- Reflect on how it made them feel
- Identify emotionally charged wording
- Reveal:
  - Intended audience
  - Emotional manipulation technique
  - Highlighted emotional phrases
  - Neutral rewritten version
  - Key learning takeaway
