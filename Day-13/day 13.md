# Day 13: AI Job Search Assistant

## Overview

For Day 13 of my 60 Days of AI Learning Journey, I explored how AI can be used as a career discovery and planning tool.

Using Claude and Indeed integration, I created a professional profile, defined job search criteria, discovered relevant opportunities, and received AI-generated insights about skill gaps and market trends.

## Objective

* Create a professional profile
* Define career interests and preferences
* Discover relevant opportunities
* Identify skills currently in demand
* Understand future growth areas in psychology

## My Professional Profile

* Second-Year B.A. Psychology Student
* Interested in Clinical Psychology, Counseling Psychology, and Mental Health
* Exploring internship and career opportunities
* Building AI and technology skills alongside psychology

## Skills Identified as Valuable

The AI analysis highlighted several skills that are commonly sought in psychology-related roles:

* Psychological Assessment
* Counseling Skills
* Case Documentation
* Research Methodology
* Data Interpretation
* Evidence-Based Practice

## Skill Development Areas

The analysis suggested focusing on:

* Practical Exposure to Mental Health Settings
* Research Experience
* Assessment Techniques
* Documentation and Reporting Skills
* Supervised Clinical Learning

## Key Market Insights

* Demand for mental health professionals continues to grow.
* Teletherapy and online mental health services are expanding rapidly.
* Research experience can strengthen future academic and professional opportunities.
* Combining psychology knowledge with technology skills can create unique career advantages.
