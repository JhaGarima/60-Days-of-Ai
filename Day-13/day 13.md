# Day 13: AI Job Search Assistant

## Overview

Today, I explored how AI can be used as a personalized career assistant by connecting Claude with Indeed and using structured prompts to create a professional profile, define job preferences, discover relevant opportunities, and analyze the current job market.

## Objective

Use AI to:

* Build a professional profile
* Define career preferences
* Discover relevant psychology opportunities
* Identify skill gaps
* Analyze market trends and hiring demand

## Professional Profile

* Psychology graduate from India
* Interested in Clinical Psychology, Counseling Psychology, Mental Health, and Psychological Research
* Preparing for postgraduate studies
* Skills: Communication, empathy, critical thinking, content creation, AI tools (ChatGPT, Claude)

## Job Search Criteria

### Target Roles

* Psychology Intern
* Clinical Psychology Intern
* Counseling Psychology Intern
* Mental Health Intern
* Research Intern
* Psychology Research Assistant

### Preferred Organizations

* Hospitals
* Mental Health Clinics
* Universities
* Research Institutes
* NGOs
* Healthcare & Wellness Organizations

### Preferences

* Remote, Hybrid, or Onsite
* Learning-focused opportunities
* Roles suitable for recent graduates
* Posted within the last 30 days

## AI Job Discovery Results

### Top Opportunities Identified

1. Mental Health Providers (MindFi)
2. Psychologist Job (Mantra Care)
3. Consultant Psychologist (Koott Wellness)
4. Laboratory Assistant - Psychology (NMIMS)
5. Quantitative Marketing Research Assistant (IIM Udaipur)
6. Assistant Editor (Wiley)

## Skill Gap Analysis

The analysis highlighted several areas that are commonly valued in psychology careers:

* Psychological assessment exposure
* Case documentation skills
* Counseling and intervention experience
* Research methodology
* Data analysis and interpretation
* Evidence-based practice
* Clinical observation skills

## Market Insights

Key trends identified:

* Growing demand for teletherapy and remote mental health services
* Increased opportunities in community mental health
* Research experience remains valuable for postgraduate admissions
* Mental health startups and digital wellness platforms are actively hiring
* Communication and technology skills can provide a competitive advantage

## Key Takeaways

* AI can significantly reduce the time required to discover relevant opportunities.
* Structured prompting improves the quality of career recommendations.
* Psychology graduates can benefit from combining domain knowledge with technology skills.
* Skill-gap analysis provides a clear roadmap for professional development.

This project demonstrated how AI can act as a personalized career assistant, helping transform broad career goals into actionable opportunities, skill-development plans, and market insights.
