# Day 11: ATS Resume Optimization Challenge

## Objective

Optimize my resume for a Clinical Psychology Intern position using Claude's ATS Resume Optimizer and analyze its alignment with a target job description.

## Target Role

Clinical Psychology Intern

## Job Description Highlights

The role focuses on supporting mental health professionals through psychological assessments, behavioral observations, case documentation, mental health research, psychoeducation initiatives, and ethical clinical practice.

## Process Followed

1. Uploaded my existing resume to Claude.
2. Provided the Clinical Psychology Intern job description.
3. Used an ATS Resume Optimizer prompt.
4. Generated:

   * ATS Match Score
   * Gap Analysis
   * Missing Keywords
   * Missing Skills
   * Resume Improvement Suggestions
5. Reviewed ATS recommendations.
6. Generated an optimized version of the resume while maintaining factual accuracy.
7. Compared the original and optimized resumes.

## Key Learnings

* ATS systems prioritize relevant keywords from the job description.
* Clinical psychology roles emphasize assessment, documentation, ethics, communication, and research skills.
* Resume sections should be clearly organized for ATS readability.
* Aligning skills and experiences with the target role improves ATS compatibility.
* Strong professional summaries help communicate career goals effectively.


## Outcome

Successfully optimized my resume for a Clinical Psychology Intern position and learned how Applicant Tracking Systems evaluate resumes based on job-specific keywords, skills, and formatting.
