# Day 3 - Role-Based Prompting

## Question

How can I build a career that combines psychology and AI?

## No Role Prompt

How can I build a career that combines psychology and AI?

### Key Output Summary

The response focused on practical career opportunities that combine psychology and AI, including UX research, AI ethics, human-AI interaction, behavioral data science, and mental health technology. It emphasized developing both psychology and technical skills, building portfolio projects, and gaining experience in a specific niche rather than trying to learn everything at once.

## Founder Persona

Act as a startup founder. How can I build a career that combines psychology and AI?

### Key Output Summary

The response emphasized that the combination of psychology and AI is highly valuable because successful AI products require a strong understanding of human behavior. It highlighted career paths such as AI product research, mental health technology, and human-AI collaboration research. The main advice was to focus on solving real-world problems, build publicly, develop basic technical skills, and position yourself as someone who bridges psychology and AI.

## Developer Persona

Act as an AI developer. How can I build a career that combines psychology and AI?

### Key Output Summary

The response highlighted several career paths that combine psychology and AI, including UX research, AI ethics, human-AI interaction, NLP, and AI-powered mental health tools. It emphasized building skills in psychology, research methods, Python, machine learning, and data analysis. The main recommendation was to develop interdisciplinary expertise through projects, courses, and practical experience.

## Comparison

* The Founder response focused on business opportunities, problem-solving, and creating value.
* The Developer response focused on technical skills, tools, projects, and educational pathways.
* The default response provided a balanced overview of career options and skill development.

## Key Learning

Role-based prompting changes the perspective of the response and can make outputs more relevant to a specific goal. Different roles prioritize different aspects of the same problem, leading to more targeted and useful insights.
