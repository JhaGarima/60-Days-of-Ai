Fuel Profiles

EV ⚡ ₹0.41/km
✅ Lowest running cost — ₹0.41/km vs ₹2.45 petrol ✅ Near-zero tailpipe emissions in daily use ❌ 4–6 hr charge time; range anxiety on long trips ❌ Battery replacement cost (₹25–40k) after 5–7y
🚗 Best for: Urban/suburban daily commutes with home charging access
Petrol ₹2.45/km
✅ Widest refuelling network; 2–3 min fill ✅ Lowest purchase price and highest resale value ❌ Highest fuel cost per km among common fuels ❌ Moderate CO₂ ~105 g/km; emission norms tightening
🚗 Best for: Occasional long-distance travellers; multi-city usage
Diesel ₹1.82/km
✅ Better fuel efficiency; suits high-km users (>2000/mo) ✅ Higher torque for highway and loaded driving ❌ Higher NOx/particulates; BS6 DPF adds maint cost ❌ Banned in some Delhi-NCR zones; higher servicing
🚗 Best for: High-mileage highway users outside urban restrictions
CNG ₹1.15/km
✅ 50% cheaper than petrol; good NCR station density ✅ Lower CO₂ and particulates vs petrol/diesel ❌ 3–10 min fill + queues at peak hours ❌ Boot space lost to cylinder; lower payload
🚗 Best for: High-km city cabs, autos, and budget fleet operators
E85 ₹1.78/km
✅ ~26% pump price saving vs petrol (₹65 vs ₹105/L) ✅ Lower CO₂ lifecycle vs pure petrol; renewable content ❌ 20–25% mileage drop wipes out pump savings ❌ Limited to flex-fuel vehicles; sparse pump network
🚗 Best for: Flex-fuel owners near E85 pumps who drive short city routes
