# Day 14: AI Job Red Flag Detector

## Objective

Today I used AI to evaluate a job opportunity before applying. The goal was to identify potential risks, hidden red flags, and positive signals that may not be obvious from a job description alone.

## Job Analyzed

**Role:** Clinical Psychology Intern
**Company:** YourDOST

## Analysis Summary

### Overall Risk Score

**20/100 (Low Risk)**

### Positive Signals

* Requirements are appropriate for an internship.
* No unrealistic experience expectations.
* Strong emphasis on mentorship and learning.
* Exposure to psychologists and mental health professionals.
* Established company with operations since 2014.
* Mission-driven work focused on mental health and emotional wellness.
* No hustle-culture or "rockstar employee" language.

### Potential Risks

* Stipend/compensation not mentioned.
* Supervision structure not clearly defined.
* Administrative responsibilities may vary.
* Work arrangement (remote/hybrid/on-site) not specified.

### Final Verdict

✅ Apply

## Key Learnings

* Not all job descriptions contain major red flags.
* Compensation transparency is important.
* Supervision quality matters in psychology internships.
* Company longevity can indicate stability.
* Asking targeted interview questions helps reduce uncertainty.
* AI can assist in evaluating opportunities before investing time in applications.

## Smart Interview Questions Generated

1. What does a typical week look like for interns?
2. How is supervision structured?
3. Is the role remote, hybrid, or in-office?
4. Are there any stipend or compensation benefits?
5. What outcomes are expected by the end of the internship?

## Reflection

This exercise showed how AI can be used as a decision-support tool rather than just a content-generation tool. By evaluating both the company and the role, I learned how to identify important factors that contribute to a healthy learning environment and career growth.
