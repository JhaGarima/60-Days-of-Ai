# Day 4 - Chain-of-Thought Prompting Career Roadmap

## Objective

The objective of this task was to use Chain-of-Thought prompting in Claude AI to generate a personalized career roadmap based on my interests, skills, and long-term career goals.

## Prompting Process

1. Studied the provided resources on Chain-of-Thought Prompting.

3. Answered the career-related questions.
4. Generated a personalized AI career roadmap.
5. Reviewed the roadmap and identified key insights.
