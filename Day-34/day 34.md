##  Overview

**Marketing Detective** is an immersive, detective-themed web application that transforms marketing analysis into an interactive investigation. Instead of reading case studies, users become the lead investigator, uncovering clues, organizing evidence, identifying strategic mistakes, and learning how successful marketing decisions are made.

Every investigation presents a unique fictional company with detailed campaign data, requiring users to analyze metrics, customer feedback, channel performance, and marketing strategy before reaching a final verdict.

#  How It Works

### 1. Accept Your Assignment
Receive a fictional marketing campaign requiring investigation.

### 2. Open the Investigation Board
Review campaign objectives, audience, channels, metrics, and supporting evidence.

### 3. Organize the Evidence
Drag and arrange evidence cards while building your understanding of the campaign.

### 4. Solve the Mystery
Identify the primary marketing mistake responsible for the campaign outcome.

### 5. Reveal the Verdict
Receive the expert explanation and compare your reasoning.

### 6. Study the Learning Report
Review practical marketing lessons and recommended improvements.

### 7. Replay
Generate a brand-new investigation with a different marketing case.
