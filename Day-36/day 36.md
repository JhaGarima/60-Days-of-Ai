## Overview

**Cognitive Pattern Explorer** is an interactive psychology-inspired web application that transforms self-reflection into a calm, exploratory experience rather than a traditional personality test.

Instead of assigning rigid labels, users navigate realistic scenarios, prioritize values through drag-and-drop activities, and map their natural decision-making process. The application then generates a personalized Reflection Journal that highlights thinking tendencies using descriptive, educational language.

The project is designed entirely with **HTML, CSS, and Vanilla JavaScript** in a **single offline HTML file**, making it lightweight, portable, and accessible.

## Chapters

### Chapter 1 — Discover Your Thinking Style

Explore realistic situations and choose the response that feels most natural. Every decision contributes toward understanding your overall thinking tendencies.

---

### Chapter 2 — Choose Your Priorities

Arrange important decision-making values using drag-and-drop interactions.

Examples include:

- Logic
- Feelings
- Curiosity
- Evidence
- Creativity
- Speed
- Certainty

---

### Chapter 3 — Map Your Thinking

Organize thinking steps into the sequence that best reflects how you naturally approach challenges.

Possible stages include:

- Observe
- Reflect
- Analyze
- Imagine
- Decide
- Act
- Review

---

## Thinking Tendencies

The application explores five educational thinking tendencies:

- Analytical Thinker
- Emotional Intuitive
- Overthinking Loop Style
- Action-First Decision Maker
- Balanced Reflective Thinker

These are presented as reflective observations rather than fixed personality labels.

---

## Reflection Journal

At the end of the experience, users receive a personalized journal containing:

- Percentage breakdown
- Dominant thinking tendencies
- Personalized observations
- Reflection prompts
- Strengths
- Areas for future growth

The language remains educational, supportive, and intentionally avoids clinical assessment.

## Getting Started

1. Download the repository.
2. Open `index.html` in any modern browser.
3. Choose Calm Mode or Stress Mode.
4. Complete all three chapters.
5. Review your personalized Reflection Journal.

No installation, dependencies, or internet connection are required.

## Disclaimer

**Cognitive Pattern Explorer** is intended for educational and self-reflection purposes only.

It does **not** diagnose, assess, or provide treatment for any psychological or mental health condition. All insights are generated from user interactions using reflective language and should not be interpreted as professional advice.
