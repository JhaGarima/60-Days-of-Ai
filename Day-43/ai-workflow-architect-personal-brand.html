Workflow Summary

This workflow turns personal brand growth into a repeatable weekly system rather than a one-off content push. You define a durable strategic foundation once, then run a lightweight loop every week: plan one core idea, produce it once in its native long-form format, repurpose it across four to five platforms with format-specific edits, schedule the batch, spend focused time in the replies and DMs it generates, and close the week by reading the three numbers that actually predict growth. The AI tools throughout are chosen to remove the slowest steps — writing, reformatting, and design — while the judgment (choosing the idea, the voice, and who to reply to) stays with you.
