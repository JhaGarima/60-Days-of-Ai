# Day 21: Digital Privacy Dashboard

## Overview
This project explores how personal data is distributed across digital platforms and how privacy risks can be analyzed through an interactive dashboard. The dashboard visualizes digital footprint metrics, exposure patterns, privacy risks, and data value assessments using a sample dataset.

## Objectives
- Analyze an individual's digital footprint.
- Measure privacy and exposure levels.
- Identify high-risk areas of personal data exposure.
- Visualize data distribution across platforms.
- Explore privacy improvement strategies through simulation.

## Features
- Digital Footprint Score
- Privacy Score Analysis
- Exposure Heatmap
- Company Exposure Ranking
- Digital Twin Profile
- Risk Radar Visualization
- Most Valuable Data Assets Analysis
- Privacy Improvement Simulator
- Final Privacy Assessment

## Key Findings
- Different platforms collect varying amounts of personal information.
- Privacy risks increase as more sensitive data is shared across multiple services.
- Certain data categories contribute more significantly to exposure levels.
- Privacy settings and data-sharing choices can substantially improve overall privacy scores.

## What I Learned
- How digital footprints are created and measured.
- The relationship between data sharing and privacy risk.
- Methods for visualizing privacy-related metrics using dashboards.
- The importance of analyzing data exposure across multiple platforms.
- How interactive dashboards can help communicate privacy insights effectively.
