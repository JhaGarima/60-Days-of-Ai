Privacy Improvement Plan
1
Audit location permissions across all 15 apps
Go to Settings → Privacy → Location Services. Set most apps to "While Using" or "Never". Estimated reduction: 60% of passive location leakage. Google Pay and Maps are exceptions where location is core functionality.
High Impact · 10 min
2
Enable App Tracking Transparency on iOS
Settings → Privacy → Tracking → turn off "Allow Apps to Request to Track." This breaks the ad-tech identifier (IDFA) that lets Instagram, TikTok, and others connect your activity across apps.
High Impact · 2 min
3
Replace WhatsApp with Signal for sensitive conversations
WhatsApp's end-to-end encryption protects message content, but metadata (who you talk to, when, how often) flows to Meta. Signal collects almost none of this.
High Impact · 15 min
4
Replace Google Search with DuckDuckGo or Brave Search
Your search history is your most commercially sensitive data. Switching search engines is the single highest-ROI privacy action — no sign-in required, and results quality is now comparable.
High Impact · 5 min
5
Review and delete Google Photos face grouping
Google Photos uses facial recognition to group photos. In Google Photos → Settings → Group similar faces → turn off. Consider whether cloud photo storage is necessary or whether local backup suffices.
Medium Impact · 10 min
6
Review TikTok and PUBG permissions deeply
Both apps have historically requested broad device access. Check clipboard, contacts, microphone, and camera permissions. Consider whether benefits outweigh the data trade-off. TikTok's data residency outside India is a specific concern.
Medium Impact · 15 min
