Part 2 — the rebuild

Every change explained, so you learn the strategy, not just the words.

Headline — 3 options

Psychology Student | AI × Human Behavior Research | Campus Ambassador, AB Talks
Keyword-optimized — front-loads terms recruiters actually search (Psychology, AI, Research, Campus Ambassador) so you surface in more results. Use this if your goal is being found.
Turning psychology into human-centered AI insight | Building in public, one AI tool a day
Value-proposition style — leads with what you offer, not your job title. Use this if you want people to understand your angle the instant they land on your profile.
Psychology + AI | Documenting 60 days of building AI tools | Campus Ambassador, AB Talks
Authority style — the "60 days" number is a concrete, unusual proof point most students don't have. Use this if your goal is thought leadership — it invites the "wait, what is this?" click.
