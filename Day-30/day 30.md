# Day 30 - AI Supply Chain Optimizer

## 📌 Overview

Built an AI-powered **Supply Chain Optimizer** that simulates real-world business decision making. The application generates a random company profile and allows users to optimize different aspects of its supply chain while observing the impact of each decision on key business metrics.

The project was generated using AI and exported as a single HTML application.

## ✨ Features

- Randomly generated company profiles
- Interactive supply chain simulation
- Factory location selection
- Warehouse strategy optimization
- Transportation method selection
- Inventory strategy planning
- Live business metrics updates
- Overall Supply Chain Score
- AI-generated strengths and weaknesses
- Risk analysis
- Personalized improvement recommendations
- Responsive UI

## 📚 What I Learned

- How different supply chain decisions influence business performance.
- The relationship between inventory, transportation, warehousing, and manufacturing.
- Designing interactive business simulations with AI.
- Structuring a complete web application from a single prompt.
- Using AI to rapidly prototype educational tools.


## 🚀 Key Takeaways

This project demonstrates how AI can be used to build practical learning tools that simplify complex business concepts. Instead of reading about supply chain optimization, users can experiment with different strategies and instantly visualize their impact on operational performance.
