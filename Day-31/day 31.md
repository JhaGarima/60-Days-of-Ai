# AI Supply Chain Control Tower

An AI-powered interactive operations simulator where you take on the role of the **Head of Operations** for a global supply chain.

As operational incidents occur in real time, your objective is to prioritize issues, make strategic decisions, and maintain healthy business KPIs before the timer runs out.

This project demonstrates how AI can be used to create engaging business simulations that teach operational thinking, prioritization, and decision-making.

---

## Objective

Run an efficient supply chain by responding to unexpected disruptions while balancing customer satisfaction, operational costs, inventory health, and service levels.

Every choice has consequences, making each playthrough different.

---

## Features

- Real-time KPI dashboard
- Randomly generated operational incidents
- Decision-based gameplay
- 3-minute simulation challenge
- Dynamic business metrics
- Performance grading system
- Live event log
- Priority-based incident management
- Pause functionality
- Sound toggle (UI)
- Instructions modal
- Fully responsive interface
- Replay support

---

## KPIs Tracked

Throughout the simulation, every decision affects key business metrics:

- Service Level
- Customer Satisfaction
- Inventory Health
- Transportation Efficiency
- Operating Cost
- Revenue Protected
- Overall Score
- Remaining Time

---

## Operational Scenarios

The simulator generates realistic supply chain disruptions, including:

- Port Congestion
- Supplier Delays
- Truck Breakdowns
- Warehouse Stockouts
- Customs Inspections
- Demand Spikes
- Factory Machine Failures
- Weather Disruptions
- Inventory Count Errors
- Damaged Shipments

Each incident includes:

- Priority level
- Business impact
- Time remaining
- Operational description

---

## Decision System

For every incident, choose the most appropriate response:

- Expedite Shipment
- Use Backup Supplier
- Reroute Trucks
- Increase Production
- Transfer Inventory
- Approve Air Freight
- Delay Decision
- Ignore

Some decisions produce immediate effects, while others influence future events, encouraging strategic thinking instead of simple reaction.

---

## Gameplay

1. Start the simulation.
2. Monitor the live KPI dashboard.
3. Prioritize incoming operational alerts.
4. Select the best action for each incident.
5. Observe how your decisions affect business performance.
6. Complete the challenge before time expires.
7. Review your final operational report and performance grade.

---

## Why I Built This

Modern supply chains depend on making fast, informed decisions during uncertainty.

Instead of creating a static dashboard, this project transforms those challenges into an interactive simulation where users experience how different operational choices impact overall business performance.

It demonstrates how AI-generated applications can make complex business concepts more engaging, practical, and accessible.
