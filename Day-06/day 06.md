## ATS Analysis

### Factors That Improved the ATS Score

* Added clear and standardized section headings.
* Improved keyword alignment with target domains.
* Enhanced professional summary for clarity and relevance.
* Reduced redundancy across sections.
* Improved readability with concise bullet points.
* Organized information in a recruiter-friendly structure.
* Strengthened action-oriented language.

### Key Learning

ATS optimization is not about adding random keywords. It is about presenting genuine skills, experiences, and qualifications in a structured format that both recruiters and ATS systems can easily understand.

## Outcome

* Learned how ATS systems screen resumes.
* Improved understanding of resume structure and keyword optimization.
* Created a more professional and ATS-friendly resume.
* Gained insight into how small formatting and wording changes can improve resume performance.

## Reflection

This exercise showed me that a resume is more than a list of qualifications. The way information is structured, worded, and presented plays a major role in whether it reaches a recruiter. Understanding ATS fundamentals will help me create stronger applications in the future.
