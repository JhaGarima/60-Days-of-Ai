# Day 23/60 - Customer & MVP Blueprint

## Overview
Today, I worked on creating a Customer & MVP Blueprint for a startup idea using customer research and product management frameworks. The goal was to better understand target customers, identify their challenges, prioritize product features, and evaluate the overall feasibility of the idea before building an MVP.

## What Was Covered

### Customer Research
- Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
- Buyer Persona Analysis
- Top Customer Pain Points
- Customer Journey Mapping
- Customer Objections & Buying Triggers

### Product Planning
- MVP Feature Recommendations
- MoSCoW Prioritization
- Pricing Hypothesis
- Risk Analysis
- Product-Market Fit Evaluation

### Validation Metrics
- Customer Clarity Score
- Problem Severity Score
- PMF Potential Score
- MVP Readiness Score
- Demand Signal Assessment

## Key Learnings
- Understanding customer problems is more important than building features quickly.
- An MVP should focus on solving the most critical customer pain points.
- Customer journeys help uncover opportunities to improve user experience.
- Prioritization frameworks prevent unnecessary feature additions.
- Early validation helps reduce business and product risks.
- Strong customer research increases the chances of achieving product-market fit.

## Deliverables
- Customer & MVP Blueprint Report
- Customer Journey Analysis
- MVP Recommendation Framework
- MoSCoW Prioritization Matrix
- Pricing Hypothesis
- Risk Assessment
- Founder Action Sheet

## Outcome
Developed a structured framework that connects customer needs with product decisions, helping create a clear roadmap for MVP development and future validation efforts.
