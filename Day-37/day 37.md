## Overview

**Task Compass** is an interactive workplace simulation game designed to teach how work flows through real organizations. Instead of testing job knowledge, it helps players understand ownership, delegation, collaboration, escalation, and workflow thinking through realistic scenarios.

The experience feels more like managing a real workplace than taking a quiz, with three progressively challenging stages that mirror how organizations operate.

---

## Features

- Workplace selection before starting (Tech Company, Startup, Corporate Office, Hospital, School, Retail, Café, Manufacturing, Media Agency, Freelancer, or Custom)
- Premium glassmorphism dark UI with animated gradients
- Responsive design for desktop and mobile
- Drag-and-drop interactions throughout the experience
- Smooth animations, hover effects, and progress tracking
- Offline functionality with no external libraries or APIs

---

## Stage 1 — Who Owns This?

Players receive realistic workplace situations and identify the primary owner by dragging the appropriate role into the ownership area.

Each explanation includes:
- Primary Owner
- Supporting Roles
- Why ownership belongs to that role

The emphasis is on understanding responsibility rather than simply getting the answer right.

---

## Stage 2 — Task Routing

Players organize how work should move through an organization.

Tasks are routed by arranging roles into the correct workflow, demonstrating how information and responsibilities pass between teams.

After submission, the workflow is animated and accompanied by an explanation of why that routing is commonly used.

---

## Stage 3 — Collaboration Challenge

Players solve larger organizational problems by selecting multiple teams that should collaborate.

Each scenario provides:
- Primary Owner
- Supporting Teams
- Communication Flow
- Detailed reasoning

This stage highlights that complex workplace problems are usually solved collaboratively.

---

## Learning Dashboard

Instead of traditional scores, the application evaluates learning across four dimensions:

- Ownership
- Delegation
- Collaboration
- Workflow Thinking

Results are presented through an animated chart that reflects the player's understanding.

---

## Reflection

At the end of the simulation, users receive personalized feedback describing:

- Areas of strong understanding
- Situations where responsibility was over-assigned
- Opportunities to improve collaboration
- A practical insight into how organizations function

## Learning Outcome

Task Compass demonstrates that effective organizations depend on clear ownership, structured workflows, and strong collaboration. Rather than focusing on job titles, it encourages systems thinking by showing how people, roles, and departments work together to solve real-world problems.
